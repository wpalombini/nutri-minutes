# Assets and licences

Every font, icon and image used in the product, with where it comes from and its licence. Only assets whose licence allows commercial use and modification are listed here. Never CC BY-NC.

## Fonts

Both fonts are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL), which allows commercial use, modification, embedding and self-hosting. The licence must travel with the font files, and the fonts can't be sold on their own. Neither font declares a Reserved Font Name, so subsetting them for the web is allowed without renaming. Both will be self-hosted by the app, not loaded from a third-party font service.
